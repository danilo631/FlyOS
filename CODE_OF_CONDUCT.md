# Fly OS Community Conduct

Fly OS welcomes technical disagreement and expects respectful collaboration. Discuss code, design and evidence rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and deliberate publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Security reports that could put users at risk should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being publicly weaponized. Maintainers may hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Project decisions should be documented with technical reasons, compatibility constraints and user impact whenever practical. Contributors are encouraged to challenge ideas constructively and to make room for newcomers to participate.
