# Third-party components

Fly OS project-specific code is GPL-3.0-or-later. The distribution integrates independent open-source projects that keep their own licenses and trademarks, including Ubuntu, Debian tooling, the Linux kernel, KDE Plasma/KWin/LayerShellQt, Qt, Wine, Flatpak, XDG Desktop Portal, fwupd, Snapper, systemd-zram-generator, Feral GameMode, Valve Gamescope and MangoHud.

Fly OS does not relicense those projects. Release images must preserve the copyright/license files supplied by their Ubuntu packages.

Apple is not a Fly OS dependency. Fly OS may adopt general product-design principles such as consistency, restrained motion and integration, but it does not distribute Apple code, logos, icons, fonts or proprietary assets.
